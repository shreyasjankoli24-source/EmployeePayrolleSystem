About Brew Haven Coffee

Our Story

Founded in 2026 by Paras Gawand, our coffee shop began with a passion for ethically sourced beans and a love for community spaces.

We are passionate about serving freshly brewed coffee sourced from the finest beans around the world.

We bring ethically sourced beans and eco-friendly practices to every cup.

Our Mission and Vision

To craft exceptional coffee experiences by sourcing the finest beans, brewing with passion, and creating a welcoming space where every sip feels like home.

Mission: To serve ethically sourced, freshly roasted coffee with warmth and care.

Vision: To be Mumbai's favorite neighborhood café, where quality meets comfort.

Our Coffee Sourcing

We source beans from Darjeeling, Assam, and Meghalaya, ensuring fair trade and sustainable practices.

Our partnerships with farmers guarantee that every cup supports communities and promotes environmental stewardship.
